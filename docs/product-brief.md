# Meridian Product Brief

## One-Line Product

Meridian converts a Toronto home's list price into the real 10-year cost of ownership using public data, spatial risk checks, and deterministic mortgage modeling.

## Problem

Home buyers anchor on list price, but ownership cost depends on hidden variables that are hard to discover quickly:

- double land transfer tax in Toronto
- property tax over time
- flood exposure
- heritage restrictions
- building condition risk
- nearby development pressure
- mortgage insurance and renewal risk
- transportation cost differences by location

These costs are fragmented across city websites, open datasets, and personal finance rules. Buyers rarely see them in one place before making decisions.

## Solution

Meridian accepts an address, list price, and buyer profile, then produces:

- a 10-year ownership cost estimate
- a transparent cost breakdown
- red/yellow/green risk flags
- a transit dividend or mobility savings signal
- buyer-profile-specific financial program signals

## User

Primary user:

- first-time Toronto home buyer

Secondary users:

- investors
- downsizers
- real estate advisors or mortgage brokers using it as a decision aid

## Why This Is Strong For The Hackathon

- clear economic systems fit
- strong City of Toronto open-data story
- auditable deterministic logic for the important calculations
- local-first privacy-preserving angle on NVIDIA hardware
- strong demo contrast: list price versus true cost

## Product Positioning

This is not an appraisal tool.

This is not a lending decision engine.

This is not an insurance quote system.

This is a decision-support product that exposes hidden ownership costs and risk signals early enough to influence a purchase decision.

## Key Insight

The updated concept is better than the original because mortgage cost should be treated as a first-class deterministic layer, not as optional narrative around the result. If Meridian claims "true cost of ownership," mortgage renewal risk, default-insured borrowing cost, and transport-cost tradeoffs materially belong in the calculation stack.
